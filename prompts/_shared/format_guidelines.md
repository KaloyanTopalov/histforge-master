Format rules for output prose:

- Third-person narration only. Never "we", "our", "us", "you", or "your".
- No section headings, no bullet points, no numbered lists in the prose output.
- No line breaks inside paragraphs. One paragraph per logical beat; paragraphs separated by a single blank line.
- No meta-commentary. Do not reference "this video", "the audience", "the story", or "the narrator".
- No introductions, no conclusions, no "as we will see". Start in the scene, end in the scene.
- Plain, concrete prose. Favor short sentences over long ones. Vary rhythm.
- Output only the requested content. No preamble, no explanations, no trailing notes.
