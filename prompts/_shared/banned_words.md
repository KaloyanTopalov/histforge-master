Do NOT use any of the following words or phrases. They mark AI-generated prose and break immersion:

- delve, delved, delving
- tapestry, rich tapestry
- navigate, navigated, navigating (as metaphor)
- embark, embarked, embarking
- unveil, unveiled, unveiling
- pivotal, seminal
- testament to, stands as a testament
- intricate, intricately
- landscape (as metaphor for situation)
- realm (as metaphor)
- in the annals of
- in conclusion, in summary
- it is important to note
- moreover, furthermore (use plain transitions)
- nuanced, multifaceted

Prefer plain concrete verbs and nouns. If the sentence uses a metaphor, make sure the metaphor is load-bearing and specific, not decorative.
