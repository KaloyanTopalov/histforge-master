Spell all numbers and dates as words, not digits. Examples:

- "1943" → "nineteen forty-three"
- "09:00" → "zero nine hundred"
- "240 men" → "two hundred and forty men"
- "3rd" → "third"
- "$50,000" → "fifty thousand dollars"

This matters because the output is read aloud by a text-to-speech voice; digits get mis-pronounced or read as separate numerals. Do not leave any digits, Roman numerals, or numeric abbreviations in the output.
